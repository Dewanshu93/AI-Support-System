## How to request access

Request access to any system, shared mailbox, distribution list or file share by raising an
**access request ticket** through the Service Desk. Include:

- the system or resource name,
- the level of access needed (for example read-only or editor),
- the business reason, and
- for temporary needs, the date the access should end.

The Service Desk cannot grant access by email or chat message without a ticket.

## Approvals

| Access type                        | Approvals required                         | Target time     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------- |
| Standard business application      | Your line manager                          | 2 business days |
| Finance, payroll or HR systems     | Line manager and the system owner          | 5 business days |
| Administrator or privileged access | Line manager, system owner and IT Security | 5 business days |

Requests without the required approvals are returned to the requester. Administrator rights on your
own laptop are not granted for convenience; IT installs approved software for you instead.

## Contractors

Contractor access always has an end date: the contract end date or **6 months** from approval,
whichever comes first. Your manager can request a renewal before it expires. Contractors cannot be
given administrator or privileged access.

## Reviews and removal

System owners review who has access every quarter. Access you no longer need is removed after the
review. When you change roles, access from your old role is removed after 30 days unless your new
manager confirms you still need it.

## Leavers

All access is removed on the person's last working day.
