## Warning signs

Lithium-ion batteries in laptops, phones and power banks can fail. Treat any of these as a safety
issue:

- the battery or laptop case is **swollen, bulging or lifting** (the trackpad may stop clicking or
  the case may not close),
- the device is **unusually hot** when idle or charging,
- **smoke**, hissing, popping or a sweet or chemical smell,
- leaking fluid.

## What to do immediately

1. **Stop using the device** and save nothing further.
2. **Unplug the charger** if you can do so safely. Do not charge it again.
3. Move the device onto a hard, non-flammable surface away from paper, fabric and people. Do not
   cover it.
4. Do not press on, puncture, bend or try to remove a swollen battery.
5. If there is smoke or fire, leave the area, raise the fire alarm and call the emergency services.
   Do not try to put out a battery fire with water.

## Report it to IT

Raise an urgent hardware ticket stating that the battery is swollen, hot or smoking, or call the
Service Desk on extension **5555**. Battery safety reports are always treated as **critical**
priority. IT arranges a replacement device and safe collection of the faulty one at no cost to you.

Do not post a damaged battery or laptop yourself; damaged lithium batteries need special handling.

## Prevention

- Use only the charger supplied with the device.
- Do not leave devices charging on beds, sofas or soft furnishings.
- Keep devices out of direct sunlight and hot cars.
