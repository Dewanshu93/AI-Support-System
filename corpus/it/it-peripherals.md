## Office desks

Office desks come with a 27-inch monitor, USB-C dock, keyboard and mouse (see Standard Laptops and
Hardware Refresh). Connect your laptop to the dock with the single USB-C cable; the monitor,
keyboard, mouse, network and charging all run through it.

## Dock or monitor not working

1. Unplug the USB-C cable from the laptop, wait 10 seconds and plug it back in.
2. Check the dock's power light is on and the monitor is set to the right input (USB-C or
   DisplayPort).
3. Try another desk. If the same laptop works elsewhere, the desk equipment is faulty.

Report faulty desk equipment with a peripheral ticket that includes the desk number.

## Requesting peripherals

You can request a replacement keyboard, mouse, headset or laptop charger with a peripheral ticket.
Items are delivered to your office desk or collected from the IT desk within 3 business days.

Ergonomic equipment (for example vertical mice or split keyboards) is provided after a workstation
assessment arranged through Facilities.

Equipment for working from home is not covered by this guide.

## Rules

- Do not take monitors or docks from shared desks.
- Personal USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.
