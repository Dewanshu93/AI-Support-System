## Password requirements

- At least **10 characters**.
- Must include an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a symbol.
- Must not reuse any of your last 12 passwords.

## Password expiry

Passwords **expire every 90 days**. You will be reminded 14 days before expiry. Change your password
at `password.example.com` before it expires to avoid being locked out.

## Multi-factor authentication

MFA is required for remote access (VPN) and for email on mobile devices. Use the Authenticator app
or a text-message code.

## Account lockout

After **5 failed sign-in attempts** the account locks for **30 minutes**. Contact the Service Desk
to unlock it sooner.
