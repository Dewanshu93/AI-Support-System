## Standard laptop models

Every full-time employee receives one company laptop on their first day. The model depends on the
role profile set by your manager when you are hired.

| Role profile | Model                        | Memory | Storage | Refresh cycle |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- | ------ | ------- | ------------- |
| Standard     | Dell Latitude 7450 (14-inch) | 16 GB  | 512 GB  | 4 years       |
| Developer    | MacBook Pro 14-inch (M4 Pro) | 36 GB  | 1 TB    | 3 years       |
| Design       | MacBook Pro 16-inch (M4 Max) | 48 GB  | 1 TB    | 3 years       |

Contractors do not receive company laptops; see the Contractor Device and Systems Access guide.

## Changing your role profile

If your work needs a different profile (for example you moved into an engineering role), your
manager raises a hardware request ticket explaining why. Requests outside your role profile need
approval from your department head.

## Refresh

IT contacts you when your laptop reaches the end of its refresh cycle and books a swap. You do not
need to request a refresh. Laptops that fail early are repaired or replaced under warranty; see the
Laptop Troubleshooting and Repairs guide.

## Office desks

Every bookable office desk has a 27-inch monitor, a USB-C dock, a keyboard and a mouse. Bring only
your laptop and charger.

## Returning equipment

Return all company hardware on your last working day, or when IT replaces it. Lost or stolen
laptops must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.
