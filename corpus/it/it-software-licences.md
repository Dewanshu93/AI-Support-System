## Requesting a licence

Request a licence for paid software (for example Adobe Creative Cloud, JetBrains IDEs, Visio or
Tableau) with a licence request ticket. Your line manager must approve, and the cost is charged to
your team's cost centre.

Licences are normally assigned within **3 business days** of approval.

## Unused licences

IT Asset Management reclaims licences that have not been used for **60 days**. You are emailed 7
days before a licence is reclaimed. If you still need it, reply to the email; otherwise it is
reassigned. You can request it again later.

## Contractors

Contractors can be assigned company licences for software used inside the virtual desktop. The
licence is removed when the contractor's access ends.

## Rules

- Licences are assigned to a person and must not be shared.
- Do not use personal or free "home" licences for company work.
- Leavers' licences are reclaimed on their last working day.
