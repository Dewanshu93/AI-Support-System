## Devices

Contractors **do not receive a company laptop or phone**. You work from your own device (or one
provided by your agency) and reach company systems through the **company virtual desktop**.

Your device must:

- run a supported version of Windows or macOS with the latest security updates,
- have antivirus enabled and a screen lock, and
- not be shared with other people while you are signed in.

## Connecting to the virtual desktop

1. Go to `desktop.example.com` in a browser, or install the Virtual Desktop client from that page.
2. Sign in with the company account created for you on your start date.
3. Approve the MFA push in the Authenticator app.

All company applications, files and email are used inside the virtual desktop. Contractors do not
use the GlobalProtect VPN.

## Data rules

- Do not download, copy, print or screenshot company data onto your own device.
- Copy and paste between the virtual desktop and your device is disabled.
- Store all work in the OneDrive and SharePoint locations inside the virtual desktop.

## Access period

Your access ends on your contract end date, or 6 months after approval if that is sooner (see
Access Requests). Ask your manager to request a renewal before it expires.

## Office Wi-Fi

In the office, connect your own device to `Corp-Guest` and then open the virtual desktop.
