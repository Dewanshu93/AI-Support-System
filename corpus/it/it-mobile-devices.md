## Company phones

Company phones are issued to roles that need them, such as on-call engineers, field staff and some
managers. Your manager requests one with a hardware request ticket. Company phones are enrolled in
device management and receive the company apps automatically.

## Using your own phone for work (BYOD)

You may read company email and Teams on your own phone using the **Outlook** and **Teams** apps.
When you first sign in, the apps apply app protection: company data stays inside these apps, cannot
be copied to personal apps, and is removed if you leave the company. Your personal photos and apps
are never visible to IT.

The phone must run a supported operating system version and have a screen lock.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen company phone, or a personal phone with company apps, to IT Security within
**24 hours**. IT will remotely wipe company data. For company phones, IT also blocks the SIM.

## Replacements and faults

Raise a hardware fault ticket for a broken company phone. Replacement phones are sent within 3
business days. Personal phones are not repaired or replaced by the company.

## Travel

International roaming on company phones is enabled on request at least 3 business days before you
travel.
