## Before you raise a ticket

Many laptop problems are fixed by these steps:

1. Restart the laptop (a full restart, not sleep).
2. Make sure it is plugged in with the company charger and the charging light is on.
3. Install pending updates and restart again.
4. For a frozen screen, hold the power button for 10 seconds, wait, and switch it back on.

## Reporting a hardware fault

If the laptop still does not work — for example it will not switch on, the screen is cracked or
flickering, keys do not respond, or it overheats during normal use — raise a hardware fault ticket.
Include:

- the **asset tag** (the sticker on the bottom of the laptop, starting with `AT-`),
- the laptop model,
- what happens and when it started.

For a battery that is swollen, very hot, smoking or leaking, **stop using the laptop immediately**
and follow the Laptop Battery Safety policy instead.

## Loan laptops

If your laptop must go away for repair, collect a loan laptop from the IT desk in your office. Loan
laptops are available the same day. Remote workers are sent one by next-day courier.

## Data

Files saved in OneDrive are safe during repairs. Files saved only on the laptop's local disk may be
lost if the disk is replaced, so keep work in OneDrive.

## Accidental damage

Accidental damage is repaired at no cost to you. Report it honestly in the ticket; repeated damage
is reviewed with your manager.
