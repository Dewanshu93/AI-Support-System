## Follow-Me printing

All office printers use **Follow-Me printing**. Print to the single printer queue called
`FollowMe` from any company laptop, then walk to any printer in any office and tap your building
badge on the reader to release the job. Jobs that are not released are deleted after 24 hours.

The `FollowMe` queue is installed automatically on company laptops. If it is missing, install
"FollowMe Printer" from Company Portal.

## Colour and large jobs

Printing is black and white and double-sided by default. Colour printing requires a cost centre to
be selected when you release the job. Print jobs over 200 pages should go to the print room instead.

## Problems

- **Badge not recognised**: register your badge once by tapping it and entering your company email
  on the printer screen.
- **Paper jam or toner empty**: follow the instructions on the printer screen. Report printers that
  stay out of service with the printer's name (on the label at the front).

## Confidential documents

Use Follow-Me printing for anything confidential so documents never sit unattended on a printer.
Put confidential paper waste in the locked shredding bins, not the recycling.
