## What changed in version 2.0

Version 2.0 follows current security guidance: longer passwords, **no scheduled password expiry**,
and multi-factor authentication (MFA) for every account. It replaces version 1.0, which required a
password change every 90 days.

## Password requirements

- At least **14 characters**. A passphrase of four or more unrelated words is recommended.
- No complexity rules (you do not need symbols or numbers), but the password must not appear on the
  list of known breached passwords, which the system checks automatically.
- Do not reuse your company password on any other website or service.

## When to change your password

You do **not** need to change your password on a schedule. Change it only if:

- you think someone else knows it or it may have been exposed, or
- IT Security asks you to.

## Multi-factor authentication

MFA is required for every account, including contractors. Use push approval in the Authenticator
app; text-message codes are allowed only as a backup method. Never approve a push that you did not
start yourself — report it to IT Security.

## Resetting your password or MFA

Use self-service reset at `password.example.com`. You need your registered MFA method. If you have
lost your phone or cannot complete MFA, raise an IT ticket for an identity-verified reset; the
Service Desk will call you back on the number in your profile.

## Account lockout

After **10 failed sign-in attempts** the account locks for **15 minutes** and then unlocks on its
own. Repeated lockouts are investigated by IT Security.
