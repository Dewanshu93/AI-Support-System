## What casual leave is for

Casual leave covers short, unplanned personal needs that are not illness and not a holiday: for
example a home repair, moving house, a school event or an urgent family errand. It is paid and is
separate from your annual leave and sick leave.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive **6 days of casual leave** each calendar year, available from
**January 1** (new hires receive a pro-rated allowance). Unused casual leave does **not** carry over
and is not paid out.

## How much at a time

You can take casual leave for **at most 2 working days at a time**. For longer absences, book
annual leave instead.

## Booking

Book casual leave in the Employee Hub, on the Leave page or by asking the AI Copilot. Give **at
least 1 working day's notice** where you can. In an emergency you may book it on the day: tell your
manager as well.

Casual leave needs approval. HR approves requests for your manager within **2 working days**, and
you are notified in the Employee Hub. You can cancel a request that is still waiting, or approved
leave that has not started.

## Who this applies to

This policy applies to full-time employees in the US and the UK. Contractors do not receive casual
leave; see the contractor time off policy.
