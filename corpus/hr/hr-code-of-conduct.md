## Who this applies to

The Code of Conduct applies to everyone who works for or with the company, including contractors.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to **$100 / £75** per item from a business contact,
as long as it is not cash and does not influence a business decision. Anything above this limit must
be declared in the compliance portal and approved by your manager before you accept it. Never accept
gifts from someone during an active tender or negotiation.

## Conflicts of interest

Declare any situation where your personal interests could conflict with the company's, for example
a second job, a close relative working for a supplier, or a financial interest in a competitor.
Declare it in the compliance portal within **30 days** of the conflict arising.

## Confidential information

Keep company, customer and colleague information confidential. Share it only with people who need
it for their work, and never on personal accounts or AI tools that are not approved by IT.

## Acceptable use of company systems

Company systems are for business use. Limited personal use is acceptable if it does not interfere
with work. Do not use company systems for illegal, offensive or harassing content.

## Raising concerns

If you see something that may break this Code, raise it as described in the Speak Up policy.
