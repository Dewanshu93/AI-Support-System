## Paid leave

Contractors are engaged through an agency or their own company and are **not paid by the company
for annual leave, sick days, public holidays or parental leave**. Any paid leave you are entitled
to comes from your agency or employer under your contract with them, and in the UK under the
Working Time Regulations through that employer.

The company's annual leave, sick leave, parental leave and public holiday policies apply to
full-time employees only.

## Planning time off

- Agree planned time off with your company manager at least **5 business days** in advance, so work
  can be covered.
- Tell your agency as well if your contract requires it.
- You do not book contractor time off in the HR portal; your manager records it in the project plan.

## Unplanned absence

If you are unwell or cannot work, message your company manager before your usual start time.

## Office closures

Offices are closed on company holidays. You will not be able to work on site on those days, and
hours are not billable unless your manager has agreed work in advance.

## Questions

Questions about your pay, timesheets or leave entitlement should go to your agency, not the company
HR team.
