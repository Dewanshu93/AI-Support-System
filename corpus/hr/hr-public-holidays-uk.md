## Bank holidays in 2026

UK offices follow the bank holidays for England and Wales. Full-time employees receive these
**8 paid bank holidays** in addition to their annual leave:

| Bank holiday            | Date in 2026       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------ |
| New Year's Day          | Thursday 1 January |
| Good Friday             | Friday 3 April     |
| Easter Monday           | Monday 6 April     |
| Early May bank holiday  | Monday 4 May       |
| Spring bank holiday     | Monday 25 May      |
| Summer bank holiday     | Monday 31 August   |
| Christmas Day           | Friday 25 December |
| Boxing Day (substitute) | Monday 28 December |

Boxing Day falls on a Saturday in 2026, so the substitute bank holiday is Monday 28 December.

## Scotland and Northern Ireland

Employees based in Scotland or Northern Ireland may swap up to two of the dates above for their
local bank holidays (for example St Andrew's Day or St Patrick's Day) with manager approval.

## Working on a bank holiday

If you are asked to work on a bank holiday, you receive a day off in lieu to be taken within 3
months.

## Contractors

Contractors are not paid for bank holidays; see Time Off for Contractors.
