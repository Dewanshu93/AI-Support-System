## Entitlement

Full-time US employees receive **10 paid sick days** each calendar year. The full allowance is
available on **January 1** (new hires receive a pro-rated allowance on their first day). Unused sick
days do **not** carry over and are not paid out when you leave.

Where state or local law gives more paid sick time, the law applies.

## What sick days cover

- your own illness, injury or medical appointments,
- caring for an ill family member,
- reasons covered by state safe-leave laws.

## Reporting an absence

Tell your manager before your normal start time on the first day, or as soon as you can. Record the
absence in the HR portal when you return.

## Medical certification

A doctor's note is required after **3 consecutive** sick days. For longer absences, HR will contact
you about short-term disability benefits and job-protected leave.

## Short-term disability

If you are unable to work for more than 7 consecutive days because of illness or injury, you may be
eligible for short-term disability pay at 70% of base salary for up to 26 weeks. HR will explain
how to claim.
