## Company holidays in 2026

US offices are closed and full-time employees are paid for these 10 company holidays:

| Holiday                     | Date in 2026         |
| --------------------------- | -------------------- |
| New Year's Day              | Thursday 1 January   |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day  | Monday 19 January    |
| Presidents' Day             | Monday 16 February   |
| Memorial Day                | Monday 25 May        |
| Juneteenth                  | Friday 19 June       |
| Independence Day (observed) | Friday 3 July        |
| Labor Day                   | Monday 7 September   |
| Thanksgiving Day            | Thursday 26 November |
| Day after Thanksgiving      | Friday 27 November   |
| Christmas Day               | Friday 25 December   |

Independence Day falls on a Saturday in 2026, so it is observed on Friday 3 July.

## Floating holidays

In addition, full-time employees receive **2 floating holidays** each year to use for religious,
cultural or personal days of their choice. Book them in the HR portal like vacation. Floating
holidays do not carry over.

## Working on a holiday

If your manager asks you to work on a company holiday, you receive another day off within the next
30 days, or holiday pay where your role is non-exempt.

## Contractors

Contractors are not paid for company holidays; see Time Off for Contractors.
