## Eligibility

Full-time US employees and their eligible dependants (spouse or domestic partner, and children up to
age 26) can enrol in the company's medical, dental and vision plans. Contractors are not eligible.

## Medical plans

You can choose between two medical plans:

- **PPO plan** with a $500 individual deductible and a broad provider network.
- **High-deductible health plan (HDHP)** with a $1,700 individual deductible and a Health Savings
  Account (HSA). The company contributes **$750 a year** to your HSA ($1,500 with dependants).

The company pays **85%** of the employee premium and **70%** of the premium for dependants on both
plans.

## Dental and vision

Dental and vision coverage are paid 100% by the company for employees; dependant coverage is
available at a shared cost.

## When coverage starts

You must enrol within **30 days** of your start date. Coverage begins on the **first day of the
month after your start date**. If you do not enrol within 30 days, you can next enrol during open
enrolment, unless you have a qualifying life event.

## Open enrolment and life events

Open enrolment runs from **1 to 15 November** each year for coverage starting 1 January. Outside
open enrolment you can change plans within 30 days of a qualifying life event, such as marriage,
birth or adoption, or a spouse losing coverage.
