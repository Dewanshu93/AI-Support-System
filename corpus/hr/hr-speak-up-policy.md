## Purpose

Everyone who works for the company should feel able to raise concerns about wrongdoing without
fear of retaliation. This policy explains the channels available.

## What you can raise

Suspected fraud or bribery, breaches of law or regulation, health and safety risks, data protection
breaches, and serious breaches of the Code of Conduct.

## How to raise a concern

You can use whichever channel you feel comfortable with:

- your line manager or another manager you trust,
- your HR business partner,
- the **confidential Ethics Line**, available 24 hours a day online or by phone, where you can
  report anonymously.

Personal concerns about how you are being treated at work are handled confidentially by the HR
case team.

## What happens next

Concerns are reviewed by the Compliance team within **5 working days**. Where appropriate, an
investigator is appointed. You will be told when the review is complete, although some details may
be confidential.

## No retaliation

The company does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, even
if it turns out to be mistaken. Retaliation is a disciplinary matter.
