## Automatic enrolment

Full-time UK employees are **automatically enrolled** in the company's workplace pension from their
first day. You can opt out within **one month** of being enrolled and receive a full refund of your
contributions. If you opt out, you are re-enrolled every three years as required by law, and you
can rejoin at any time.

Contractors are not enrolled in the company pension.

## Contributions

- You contribute **4%** of your basic salary.
- The company contributes **6%** of your basic salary.

Contributions are made through **salary sacrifice**, which reduces your National Insurance. You can
increase your own contribution at any time; the company contribution stays at 6%.

## Investments

Contributions go into the default lifestyle fund unless you choose other funds in the pension
provider's portal.

## Leaving the company

Your pension pot stays yours when you leave. You can leave it invested or transfer it to another
scheme.
