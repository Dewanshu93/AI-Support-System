## Who this applies to

This policy covers full-time employees based in the United Kingdom. US employees should read
Annual Leave (US). Contractors do not receive paid annual leave from the company; see Time Off for
Contractors.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive **25 days** of paid annual leave each holiday year, plus the **8 UK bank
holidays** (see Public Holidays (UK)). After **5 years** of service the entitlement rises to
**27 days** plus bank holidays.

This is more than the statutory minimum of 28 days including bank holidays. Part-time entitlement
is pro-rated.

The holiday year runs from 1 January to 31 December. If you join or leave during the year, your
entitlement is pro-rated.

## Booking leave

- Book leave in the HR portal and wait for your manager's approval before making plans.
- For a week or more, give at least **4 weeks' notice** where possible.
- Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days.

## Carry-over

You may carry over up to **5 days** into the next holiday year. Carried-over days must be taken by
**31 March**. Leave you could not take because of sickness or family leave is carried over in line
with the law.

## Leaving the company

Untaken leave is paid in your final salary. Leave taken beyond your pro-rated entitlement is
deducted from your final salary.
