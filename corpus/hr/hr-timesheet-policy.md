## Logging your time

Log the hours you work every working day in the Employee Hub, either on the timesheet page or by
asking the AI Copilot (for example, "Log 8 hours for the Banking project yesterday"). The copilot
always shows the entry for you to confirm before it is saved.

- A full working day is **8 hours**.
- Log time in steps of **half an hour**.
- You can log at most **12 hours** in one day across all projects.
- Log time only against projects you are assigned to. Internal meetings and training go to the
  internal project.

## Deadlines

Log each day by the **end of the next working day**. Complete the previous week's timesheet by
**Monday at 12:00** local time.

You can add or correct entries for up to **60 days** after the day worked. Entries added after
their pay period has closed are marked as late.

## Missing timesheets

You get a reminder for every past working day with fewer than 8 hours logged. For full-time
employees, a working day with no timesheet and no attendance record at the payroll cutoff is treated
as Loss of Pay (see the Loss of Pay policy).

## Contractors

Contractors log their hours in the same way. Their agency approves the hours weekly and pays them;
questions about contractor pay go to the agency.
