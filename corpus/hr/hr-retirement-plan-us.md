## Eligibility and enrolment

Full-time US employees can join the 401(k) plan from their **first day**. New employees are
**automatically enrolled at 3%** of eligible pay into the plan's default target-date fund unless
they choose a different rate or opt out within 30 days.

Contractors cannot join the 401(k) plan.

## Company match

The company matches:

- **100% of the first 4%** of pay you contribute, plus
- **50% of the next 2%**.

Contributing 6% of pay therefore earns the maximum match of **5%**.

## Vesting

Your own contributions and the company match are **100% vested immediately**.

## Changing your contribution

You can change your contribution rate, choose traditional (pre-tax) or Roth contributions, and pick
investments at any time in the 401(k) provider's portal. Changes take effect from the next payroll.

## Limits

Contributions are subject to the annual IRS limits. Employees aged 50 or over can make additional
catch-up contributions.
