## Who this applies to

This policy covers full-time employees based in the United States. UK employees should read
Annual Leave (UK). Contractors do not receive paid annual leave from the company; see Time Off for
Contractors.

## Entitlement

| Length of service | Paid vacation per year |
| ----------------- | ---------------------- |
| Less than 5 years | 15 days                |
| 5 years or more   | 20 days                |

Vacation accrues monthly: 1.25 days per month for 15 days a year, or 1.67 days per month for 20
days a year. New hires start accruing from their first day. Vacation is in addition to company
holidays (see Public Holidays (US)).

## Requesting vacation

- Request vacation in the HR portal before you take it.
- For more than 3 consecutive days, request at least **10 business days** in advance.
- Your manager approves or declines within 3 business days.

## Carry-over

You may carry over up to **5 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by
**March 31**; any not used by then are forfeited. Days above the carry-over limit are forfeited on
December 31.

## Leaving the company

Accrued, unused vacation is paid out in your final paycheck where state law requires it. Otherwise
it is paid out at the company's standard rate of up to 5 days.
