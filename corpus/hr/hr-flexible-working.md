## Hybrid working

Employees work in a company office at least **3 days a week** and may work from home on the other
days, unless their role requires full-time attendance. Teams agree their office days together.

## Core hours

Core hours are **10:00 to 15:00 local time**, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can start
and finish at times that suit you, as long as you work your contracted hours and attend agreed
meetings.

## Requesting other arrangements

You can ask for a different arrangement, such as compressed hours, part-time hours or a different
pattern of office days. Submit a flexible working request in the HR portal; your manager responds
within **4 weeks**.

In the UK you have a statutory right to make a flexible working request from your first day of
employment, and you can make up to two requests in any 12-month period.

## Working from another country

You may work from another country for up to **20 working days** in a calendar year, with manager
approval and HR confirmation submitted at least **2 weeks** before you travel. Longer periods are
usually not possible because of tax and immigration rules.

## Contractors

Contractors agree their working pattern and location with their company manager under their
contract.
