## When to use this process

Use the formal performance improvement process when informal feedback has not resolved a
performance concern over at least **6 weeks**. Always speak to your HR business partner before
starting it. This process is not used for conduct issues or during probation.

## Performance improvement plan (PIP)

A PIP lasts **60 days**. It must include:

- the specific performance gaps, with examples,
- measurable objectives for the period,
- the support the company will provide (training, mentoring, adjusted workload),
- check-in dates.

Share the PIP in a meeting with the employee and your HR business partner, and give the employee a
written copy.

## During the plan

Hold check-ins **every 2 weeks** and record progress in writing after each one. If the employee
raises health issues or other circumstances affecting performance, pause and involve HR.

## Outcomes

At the end of the plan, meet the employee with HR to confirm one of three outcomes: objectives met
(plan closed), plan extended once by up to 30 days, or progression to a formal capability hearing.
No outcome other than closing the plan may be decided without HR.

## Records

Keep all PIP documents in the HR case system, not in email or personal folders.
