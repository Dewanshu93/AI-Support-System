## What changed in version 3.0

From 1 January 2026, paid parental leave increases from 12 to **16 weeks**, and the eligibility
period is shortened from 6 months to **90 days** of service. Version 3.0 replaces version 2.0.

## Eligibility

Full-time US employees who have completed **90 days** of continuous service are eligible. The policy
applies equally to birth parents, non-birth parents and adoptive or foster parents. Contractors are
not covered by this policy.

## Paid leave

Eligible employees receive **16 weeks of parental leave at full base pay** for each birth, adoption
or foster placement. Leave must be taken within **12 months** of the birth or placement. It can be
taken in one block or in up to two blocks of at least 2 weeks each.

In addition, employees can take up to **4 weeks of unpaid leave** immediately after the paid leave,
with manager agreement.

## Relationship with FMLA and state programmes

Paid parental leave runs at the same time as job-protected leave under the Family and Medical Leave
Act (FMLA) where it applies. If a state paid family leave programme also pays benefits, the company
tops up the state benefit to full base pay rather than paying both in full.

## Requesting leave

Tell your manager and submit a parental leave request in the HR portal at least **30 days** before
the expected start date, or as soon as possible for unplanned arrivals. HR confirms the dates and
pay arrangements in writing.

## Benefits during leave

Health benefits and 401(k) contributions continue during paid parental leave.
