## Private medical insurance

Full-time UK employees are covered by company-paid **private medical insurance** from their first
day. It covers specialist consultations, diagnostic tests and in-patient treatment, with no excess
for employees.

You can add a partner and children to the policy. Family cover is paid by you through a monthly
salary deduction. Private medical insurance is a taxable benefit and appears on your P11D.

Contractors are not eligible for company health benefits.

## Dental cash plan

A dental and optical cash plan refunds up to **£250 a year** for dental treatment and **£150 a
year** for glasses or contact lenses. Claims are made online with a receipt.

## Employee Assistance Programme

All UK employees can use the confidential Employee Assistance Programme 24 hours a day for
counselling, legal information and financial guidance.

## Joining and changes

Employee cover starts automatically on your start date. To add family members, use the benefits
portal within **30 days** of joining or of a life event (for example marriage or the birth of a
child), or at the annual benefits window in **November**.
