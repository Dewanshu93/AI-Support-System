## Eligibility

Full-time US employees who have completed **6 months** of continuous service are eligible. The
policy applies to birth parents, non-birth parents and adoptive parents.

## Paid leave

Eligible employees receive **12 weeks of parental leave at full base pay** for each birth or
adoption. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption, in a single block.

## Requesting leave

Submit a parental leave request in the HR portal at least **60 days** before the expected start
date.

## Benefits during leave

Health benefits continue during paid parental leave.
