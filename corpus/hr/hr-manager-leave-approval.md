## Your responsibilities

As a manager you approve annual leave, floating holidays and flexible working requests for your
direct reports in the HR portal. Respond to every request within **3 working days**. Requests left
unanswered for 5 working days are escalated to your own manager.

## Approving annual leave

- Check team coverage before approving overlapping requests; approve in the order requests were
  submitted when coverage is limited.
- You may decline a request only for a clear business reason, which you must record in the portal.
- Do not approve more than **10 consecutive working days** without checking that handover is in
  place; requests over 15 working days need your department head's approval.

## Carry-over exceptions

Employees may carry over up to 5 days. Exceptions above 5 days (for example after a long project or
sickness) need approval from the **HR director** and are capped at 10 days.

## Sickness and family leave

Do not ask for medical details. Record sickness absences in the portal and contact HR if an absence
is likely to last more than 2 weeks, or for any parental leave request, so HR can confirm
entitlements.

## Leavers

Before a team member leaves, check their remaining leave balance in the portal and agree whether
leave will be taken during the notice period or paid out.
