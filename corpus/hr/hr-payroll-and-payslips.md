## Pay dates

- **US employees** are paid **semi-monthly** on the **15th** and the **last day** of each month.
- **UK employees** are paid **monthly** on the **25th** of each month.

If a pay date falls on a weekend or holiday, you are paid on the previous working day.

## Payslips

Payslips are published in the payroll portal **2 working days** before each pay date. They are not
sent by email or post. You can download payslips and year-end tax forms (W-2 in the US, P60 in the
UK) from the portal at any time.

## Changes to your details

Update your bank account, address and tax withholding (W-4 in the US) in the HR portal. Changes made
after the **5th** of the month (UK) or more than 5 working days before a pay date (US) apply from the
following pay run.

## Questions or errors

If your pay looks wrong, raise a payroll query in the HR portal with the payslip date and the
amount you think is wrong. Payroll replies within 3 working days. Underpayments are corrected in an
off-cycle payment if they are over $200 / £150, otherwise in the next pay run.

## Contractors

Contractors are paid by their agency, not through company payroll.
