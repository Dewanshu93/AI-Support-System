## Who this applies to

Full-time employees based in the UK. Statutory rights apply from day one; the company's enhanced
pay below requires **26 weeks** of continuous service by the 15th week before the expected week of
childbirth or by the date of matching for adoption. Contractors are not covered by this policy.

## Maternity and adoption leave

You can take up to **52 weeks** of maternity or adoption leave.

- Company enhanced pay: **20 weeks at full basic salary**.
- Then Statutory Maternity or Adoption Pay for the rest of the first 39 weeks.
- The final 13 weeks are unpaid.

## Paternity leave

Eligible partners can take **4 weeks of paternity leave at full basic salary** (the statutory
entitlement is 2 weeks). It must be taken within 52 weeks of the birth or placement, in blocks of at
least one week.

## Shared parental leave

Parents can share up to 50 weeks of leave and 37 weeks of pay by ending maternity or adoption leave
early. Shared parental leave is paid at the statutory rate. Give HR at least **8 weeks' notice** of
each block.

## Notice

Tell HR about a pregnancy by the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth, or within 7 days
of being matched for adoption. Submit your leave request in the HR portal.

## During leave

You keep accruing annual leave and your pension contributions continue during paid leave.
