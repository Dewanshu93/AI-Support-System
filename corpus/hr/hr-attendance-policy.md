## How attendance is recorded

Attendance is recorded automatically for every working day (Monday to Friday):

| Status  | Meaning                                                         |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Present | You badged in at a company office                               |
| Remote  | You worked from home under the hybrid working policy            |
| Leave   | Approved leave was recorded for the day                         |
| Holiday | A public or bank holiday for your country                       |
| Absent  | No badge-in, no remote sign-in and no approved leave or holiday |

## Being marked absent

A day marked **Absent** with no timesheet entry is treated as Loss of Pay at the payroll cutoff.
Your manager is told when you are marked absent on **2 consecutive working days**.

## Correcting your attendance

If you were marked absent but worked that day, log your hours in your timesheet and raise an HR
request for an attendance correction within **30 days**. If Loss of Pay was already deducted, ask
for an LOP review in the same request.

## Planned absences

Book leave in advance in the HR portal so that the day is recorded as Leave. For unplanned absence
because of illness, follow the sick leave policy for your country.
