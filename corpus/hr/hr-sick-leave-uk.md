## Company sick pay

After you pass your probation period, company sick pay is **full basic salary for up to 20 working
days** in any rolling 12-month period. After that, or during probation, you receive Statutory Sick
Pay (SSP) if you are eligible.

## Reporting an absence

Phone or message your manager before your normal start time on the first day of absence and say
when you expect to return. Keep your manager updated if the absence continues.

## Evidence

- Up to **7 calendar days**: self-certify by completing the absence form in the HR portal when you
  return.
- More than 7 calendar days: you need a **fit note** from your GP or hospital.

## Return to work

Your manager will hold a short return-to-work conversation after any absence. If you have a
condition that affects your work, HR can arrange an occupational health referral and agree
reasonable adjustments.

## Long-term absence

For absences longer than 4 weeks, HR will contact you to agree how to stay in touch and to support
your return, which may include a phased return.
