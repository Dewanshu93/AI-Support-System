## Who this applies to

This policy covers business expenses for full-time employees in the US and UK. Contractors claim
expenses through their agency under their contract.

## Limits

| Expense                | US limit                                 | UK limit                |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Meals while travelling | $65 per day                              | £45 per day             |
| Hotel, standard city   | $250 per night                           | £150 per night          |
| Hotel, high-cost city  | $350 per night (New York, San Francisco) | £200 per night (London) |
| Personal car mileage   | $0.70 per mile                           | £0.45 per mile          |
| Client entertainment   | $100 per person                          | £75 per person          |

Book flights and train travel through the company travel tool. Economy class is standard; business
class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours with director approval.

## Submitting a claim

- Submit claims in the expenses tool within **30 days** of spending.
- Attach an itemised receipt for every item over **$25 / £20**.
- Your line manager approves the claim.

Approved claims are reimbursed with the **next payroll** after approval.

## Not reimbursed

Alcohol without client entertainment, personal travel extensions, fines and parking tickets, travel
upgrades, and software or equipment bought without IT approval.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than six times a year can apply for a corporate card. Corporate card
spending follows the same limits and must be reconciled within 30 days.
